Privacy policy

This policy covers Recorder upload, a personal tool used only by its owner.

What it can reach in Google Drive

Its owner grants it Google's drive.file permission, which lets it reach only the files it creates itself in the owner's Google Drive. It cannot see, open or change any other file.

What it does with those files

It creates them and writes into them copies of the files its owner records. It does nothing else with them.

Personal data

It collects, keeps and shares no one's personal data. It sends nothing to anyone and sells nothing.

This website

This website uses no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub's own privacy statement covers what GitHub's servers record.
